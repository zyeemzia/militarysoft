SER 04

How they are built

Every program here was written to answer a specific problem in a specific office: an instructor who needs to know why a radio link failed, a training wing juggling hundreds of lessons a week, a records office waiting minutes for a report, a centre turning result sheets into decisions.

They share one discipline. They run on the equipment a unit already has, keep working when the network does not, and are tested before they reach a user. The field apps follow the same rules on a phone.
